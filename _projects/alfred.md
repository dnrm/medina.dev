# Alfred

Alfred is a territory-based management platform for Royal Prestige direct-sales networks, built to replace the chaos of coordinating over WhatsApp. Reps schedule, confirm and log demos and sales shows, with automatic reminders. Leaders coordinate recruitment interviews and inductions, and follow real-time KPIs by person, team and territory. Role-based visibility, configurable team hierarchies, leaderboards and a daily agenda keep everyone aligned. Alfred is currently in beta, with a 7-day free trial and subscription plans for leaders and distributors.
