# MedicFlow

MedicFlow is an AI assistant that streamlines clinical documentation. It listens, transcribes and generates clinical notes, cutting documentation time from 15-20 minutes to under a minute. Notes adapt to each doctor's style and specialty, and integrate with electronic health records, helping reduce burnout so doctors can focus on their patients.
