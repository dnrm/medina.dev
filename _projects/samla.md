# SAMLA

SAMLA is a technology company building AI solutions for businesses. Its customizable agents automate daily work: Samuel manages tasks, Sarah handles calls and reservations, and Julian takes care of procurement. Companies pick and configure the agents they need, improving efficiency and customer service around the clock while cutting costs.
