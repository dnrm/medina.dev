# Centro Invia

Centro Invia is a micropigmentation center in Monterrey offering microblading, micro-needling, lip micropigmentation and laser removal. The site reflects their focus on natural, long-lasting results, backed by advanced techniques, high biosafety standards and a certified specialist.
